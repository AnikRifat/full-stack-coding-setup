# Fintech

High risk by default: correctness outranks speed. Model account, ledger, transaction, transfer, beneficiary, current and available balance, hold, fee, KYC, limit, and dispute. Before an irreversible transfer, clearly show amount, recipient, fee, total debit, and timing; after it, give a receipt and reference. Prevent duplicate submits, explain whether money moved, retain auditable statements, and never hide balance semantics.
