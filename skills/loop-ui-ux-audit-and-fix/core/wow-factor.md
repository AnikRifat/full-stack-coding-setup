# Wow without novelty theater

Wow is effortless competence: clarity, disciplined craft, and one memorable moment. Put the user’s most important question or action first; strong B2B design often means “7 orders need action,” not ornament.

Use deliberate type scale, one spacing rhythm, one accent, one radius family, real content, and polished loading, empty, error, and success states. Give each product at most one signature moment: a useful visualization, satisfying interaction, typographic fingerprint, branded state set, or purposeful marketing reveal.

For conversion surfaces: above the fold explains what it is, who it is for, proof, and one CTA. Keep CTA language consistent, reduce commitment, make objections and landed cost visible early, and make mobile actions thumb reachable. Check blur test, five-second test, real content, state coverage, speed, and remove-one restraint before shipping.
