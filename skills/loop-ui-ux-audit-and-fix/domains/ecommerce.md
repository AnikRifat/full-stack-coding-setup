# Ecommerce

Model products, variants, SKU, inventory, cart, order, payment, shipment, return, customer, coupon, and review. Make available versus reserved inventory explicit. Buyer jobs are find, evaluate, trust, buy, track, and return; seller jobs are fulfill, stock, publish, price, and resolve exceptions.

Product pages prioritize gallery, price, variant availability, delivery promise, CTA, trust, details, and reviews. Checkout reduces commitment, shows landed cost and payment trust early, and recovers zero results. Seller queues prioritize today's action, order age, payment, status, courier, and bulk operations. Common failures: F-STATE, F-JOB, F-TRUST, F-CONV-FRICTION, F-DECISION.
